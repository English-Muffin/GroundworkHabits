Privacy policy

Groundwork Habits · last updated 15 November 2026

The short version

Groundwork Habits has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your habits, tasks, goals and notes stay on your device. The developer never receives, stores or sees your data.

What the app stores

Everything you enter is saved on your own device: in the app’s storage on Windows, macOS and Android, or in your browser’s storage on the web. Automatic backups are saved on your device too, or in a folder you choose.

When the app uses the internet

The app works fully offline. It only connects to the internet for features you turn on yourself:

  • Cloud sync with Google Drive or Dropbox. If you sign in, the app saves one file, groundwork.json, in your own account: in Google Drive’s hidden app folder, or in Dropbox under Apps/Groundwork Habits. The app talks to Google or Dropbox directly from your device; there is no server in between. It can only see the files it created itself, not the rest of your Drive or Dropbox. It also reads your account’s email address to show which account is connected. The sign-in tokens are stored only on your device. Signing out removes them; your file stays in your account until you delete it.
  • A data folder you pick. The app writes the same file to that folder. If the folder is synced by another app (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox), that app uploads it under its own privacy policy.
  • A calendar link you add, or Google Calendar. The app downloads that calendar to show its events as tasks. If you sign in with Google for this, the app gets read-only access to your calendars and reads only the events of your main calendar from 30 days back to 90 days ahead, plus your email address to show which account is connected. It never changes your calendar, and the events stay on your device as tasks.
  • Update checks (desktop and Android, when switched on). The app asks GitHub for the latest release.

Google user data

For cloud sync the app requests access to Google Drive’s app data folder (drive.appdata) and your email address. It uses them only to save and read your own Groundwork Habits data and to show which account is connected. For the calendar it requests read-only access to Google Calendar (calendar.readonly) and uses it only to show your events as tasks on your device. This data is never sent to the developer or anyone else, never sold, and never used for advertising. Groundwork Habits’ use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Encryption

If you set a PIN, your data, backups and exports are encrypted with it, including the copy in your cloud or data folder.

Deleting your data

Settings > Data & backups > Start fresh erases everything on the device. To remove the cloud copy and its backups, use Settings > Data & backups > Delete my data from Google Drive / Dropbox (it signs you out too). You can also delete them yourself: in Dropbox, the Apps/Groundwork Habits folder; in Google Drive, Settings > Manage apps > Groundwork Habits > Delete hidden app data. You can also remove the app’s access in your Google or Dropbox account settings.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
